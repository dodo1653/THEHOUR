How it works

01

Submit

Name, ticker, picture. Your wallet signs your coin's pump.fun launch now, against a durable nonce: a small account your wallet owns that keeps the signed launch valid until it's used. Copies of anything launched in the last hour, or already in the running, are refused.

02

Vote

Anyone votes, free, no wallet: your browser keeps a key that signs your vote. One vote per person and per connection each hour.

03

:00

At the top of the hour the relay broadcasts the winner's own signed launch. The coin goes live on pump.fun with its creator as the dev, even if they're asleep. If it can't land, the next in line launches.

04

The hall

Every hour's coin goes into the hall with its votes and its live market cap. The rest stay in the running for up to three hours; their votes start again each hour.

What it costs you. The nonce account holds about 0.0015 SOL of rent, which is yours and comes back when you close it. Pump.fun's normal launch cost is paid only if your coin wins, from your wallet at :00, with your optional first buy. The Hour takes nothing.

Why a nonce. A normal Solana transaction expires after about a minute. A durable nonce replaces that expiry with a value stored on-chain that only changes when the transaction is used, so your signed launch can wait for :00. Nobody else can change it, and nobody can alter the launch: any change breaks your signature.

If you change your mind. Advance or close your nonce account from your wallet and your signed launch can never land.
